What West Virginia Buyers Ask — And What the Compass Actually Answers

Three questions surface in almost every Compass sales conversation I have in this market. First: “Is the 4x4 real, or is it like what Toyota and Honda call 4x4?” The honest answer is that Toyota RAV4 AWD uses a system that disconnects the rear axle entirely during steady-state highway driving — it is not engaged unless the front wheels slip and a computer decides to add rear power. The Honda CR-V Real Time AWD operates similarly. The 2026 Compass Active Drive 4x4 system uses a different architecture that maintains mechanical engagement readiness permanently and can distribute up to 50% of torque rearward within half a rotation of front-wheel slip. For West Virginia terrain, that half-rotation response time is the difference between regaining traction smoothly and the brief alarming loss of control you sometimes experience in competing vehicles on ice or mud.

Second question: “My family has two kids and a dog — is the Compass actually usable inside?” The 2026 generation represents a genuine interior redesign over the 2022–2024 models. The rear seat now provides 38.1 inches of legroom — a number that matters because it puts average adults in the back seat without their knees contacting the front seatbacks. Cargo volume behind the rear seat is 27.2 cubic feet, which places it competitive with the Ford Escape and above the Honda HR-V. The one thing I always note: the optional panoramic sunroof takes a small bite out of headroom in the second row, specifically for passengers over 6’1”. If you are tall, test that configuration specifically.

Third question, and this is the one that matters most for any honest conversation: “What goes wrong with the Compass?” I have watched the previous generation accumulate two recurring issues in our service department — a transmission hesitation in certain driving conditions that Stellantis addressed via software update in 2024, and a passenger-side door seal leak reported on early 2023 production builds that was corrected mid-production run. The 2026 platform benefits from both corrections at the factory level. Terry Foden, Jr., our shop foreman and the person responsible for technician training at our location, confirmed for me that no 2026 Compass has come into our service bay with either of those historical issues. That is a data point worth knowing.
