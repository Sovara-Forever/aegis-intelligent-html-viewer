At RC CDJR in Newell, WV, the Grand Cherokee L fits families who use a third row often; everyone else is better served by the two-row model. The L is 11.4 inches longer and seats up to seven, yet tows the same 6,200 pounds. Here is how the two compare for Upper Ohio Valley families.

Two rows or three for Weirton and Steubenville families?

Start with how often the third row would be used. If it is daily carpools or three kids in car seats, the L is the honest answer.

If it is a few times a year when the grandkids visit, the two-row model keeps more cargo behind the second row. It is also 11.4 inches shorter to park and garage.

  • Choose the Grand Cherokee if you carry four or five people and want the bigger everyday cargo floor. The tradeoff is no option for extra seats.
  • Choose the Grand Cherokee L if you need six or seven seats more than occasionally. The tradeoff is a longer body and a small fuel-economy hit.

Measure your garage before you decide. In many older Weirton and Steubenville homes, 11 extra inches is the difference between fitting and not.

If you’re coming to compare them, we’re about 22 minutes from Weirton up WV-2 North and about 35 minutes from Steubenville via WV-2 North. From the city, we’re under an hour from Pittsburgh via I-376 West and US-30 West. Bring the tape measure numbers with you.

How usable is the Grand Cherokee L third row?

Jeep lists 30.3 inches of third-row legroom. That suits kids and shorter adults on short trips better than adults on a Pittsburgh commute.

The second row is where the L shines. It has 39.4 inches of legroom, and captain’s chairs make a walkway to the back row.

With the third row up you still get 17.2 cubic feet behind it, enough for a grocery run. Fold it and the L opens to 46.9 cubic feet, more than the two-row model behind its second row.

Bring your car seats to the test drive. It is far better to install them on our lot than to find a fit problem in your driveway.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L?

The Grand Cherokee seats five in two rows. The L is 11.4 inches longer, rides on a 5-inch-longer wheelbase and adds a third row. It seats six with captain’s chairs or seven with the optional bench.

What does the L stand for on a Jeep Grand Cherokee?

Jeep doesn’t publish an official meaning, but the L is the long, three-row version. It shares engines, trim names and the 6,200-pound tow rating with the two-row Grand Cherokee but stretches to 204.9 inches.

Is the Grand Cherokee L third row usable for adults?

It is best for kids and shorter trips. Jeep lists 30.3 inches of third-row legroom, compared with 39.4 inches in the second row, so adults fit for short runs rather than long drives.

Does the Grand Cherokee L have less cargo space?

Behind the third row, yes: 17.2 cubic feet. Fold it and you get 46.9 cubic feet, more than the two-row model’s 37.7. With every rear seat folded the L offers 84.6 cubic feet versus 70.8.

Are Grand Cherokee L models good cars?

For families who need a third row only some of the time, the L makes sense. It keeps Grand Cherokee towing and engines, though the longer body costs about one mpg combined with the V6.

What trim levels are available on the Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L?

Both use the 2026 trim ladder. Laredo and Laredo X run the V6, while Laredo Altitude, Limited and Summit get the Hurricane 4 Turbo. Jeep’s 2027 Grand Cherokee release adds Laredo Upland, Trailhawk and Overland.
