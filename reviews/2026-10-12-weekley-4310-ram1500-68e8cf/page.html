Questions Ohio shoppers ask before they buy

What do I actually pay beyond the Final Price at Weekley in Butler?

Weekley CDJR in Butler shows a Final Price that already includes the $398 documentary fee. Ohio tax, title, and license come on top. That $398 fee is the 2026 Ohio maximum under O.R.C. 4517.261. Sales tax follows your home county rate, not the store’s county — Richland County combined rate is 7.00% as of 2026.

Ask Erik for an itemized out-the-door worksheet on the exact VIN before you drive up US-30. Do not budget off the red number on the card alone.

Should I pick the 5.7L HEMI or the 3.0 Hurricane for Ohio driving?

Choose by the job and the budget, then drive both. If price comes first, the two 3.0 Hurricane twin-turbo Big Horns, T7339 and T7338, carry the lowest Final Prices on this list, and T7339’s Vehicle Detail Page lists 420 hp against 395 hp on the HEMI trucks’ pages, so a six doesn’t mean giving up power. If you’ve run a HEMI before and want the V8 you know, two of the 2026s have the 5.7 HEMI with eTorque, and the 2027 Big Horn V7423 and Laramie V7421 have the 5.7 HEMI; their pages don’t list eTorque. If you tow, the engine is only half the answer: look for Trailer Brake Control and the 3.92 rear axle on the card, which the Hurricane T7339 and the HEMI T7368 both list.

Drive one of each on the same day. Decide after a loaded US-30 pull and a stop-and-go loop through Mansfield — not from a spec sheet. Call 567-430-2908 and Erik will stage both.

Can I get approved if I’m younger or buying my first new truck?

Weekley CDJR in Butler will run your file through Chrysler Capital and outside banks — approval is not automatic. Not every buyer qualifies for every rate or program. Bring proof of income, housing, and insurance. A co-buyer changes the file more than a pep talk does.

Get pre-approved online before you fall in love with a color. Then shop the payment against the truck price, not the other way around.

How does my trade change the number on a new RAM in Ohio?

Ohio taxes a new truck from a licensed dealer on price minus your trade allowance. That credit matters in Richland, Ashland, and Knox County as much as the allowance itself. If you still owe more than the trade is worth, the gap rolls into the new loan unless you pay it down.

Start with a written allowance on Value Your Trade. Keep the truck price and the trade number on separate lines so nobody buries one inside the other.

Why would I buy a 2026 on this list instead of a 2027?

Pick the year by the equipment and the Final Price, not the number on the tailgate. All six trucks are on the lot today: four 2026s and two 2027s, all Big Horn and Laramie 4x4s. If you want the 3.0 Hurricane or the 5.7 HEMI with eTorque, that’s a 2026 on this list. If you want trailer brake control, a locking rear axle, and a bed package on one Big Horn, the 2027 V7423 has all three; the 2027 Laramie V7421 adds the surround-view camera and a 12-way trailer connector.

If you need a truck this week for work or winter, every truck on this list is already at 50 Main St, so you’re not waiting on an order. Line up the Final Price and the equipment you need, and I’ll have a 2026 and a 2027 ready to drive back to back; from Mansfield that’s about 23 minutes down SR-13 and SR-97, and from Mount Vernon about 25, so one visit settles it.
