Questions Ohio shoppers ask before they buy a used vehicle at Weekley

What do I pay beyond the Final Price on a used vehicle in Butler?

Weekley’s Final Price already includes the $398 documentary fee. Ohio tax, title, and license come on top. That $398 fee is the 2026 Ohio maximum under O.R.C. 4517.261. Sales tax follows your home county rate, not the store’s county — Richland County combined rate is 7.00% as of 2026.

Ask Erik for an itemized out-the-door worksheet on the exact VIN before you drive up US-30 from Mansfield, SR-13, or SR-97, or up from Mount Vernon, about 25 minutes away. Do not budget off the red number on the card alone. Use the Final Price on this page, then add tax, title, and license on paper.

Are these certified pre-owned?

No. The units on this shortlist are pre-owned vehicles on the Weekley lot. This page does not call them Certified Pre-Owned. Confirm inspection notes and any remaining factory coverage on that VIN before you buy. Written answers beat verbal ones.

If you want the full used list — including units under $20,000 — use Shop All Pre-Owned or Used Under $20,000.

Should I buy a used RAM 1500 or a used Grand Cherokee for US-30 and winter?

Take the RAM if the job is payload, a bed, and a hitch. Take the Grand Cherokee or Grand Cherokee L if the job is family seats and weather. Stock 4317 is the 2023 RAM 1500 Big Horn 5.7L V8. Stock 4374 is the 2025 Grand Cherokee L Limited 3-row. Stock 4267 is the 2022 Grand Cherokee L Limited 3-row.

Drive one of each the same day. Call 567-430-2908 and Erik will stage them. Do not pick from a spec sheet.

How does a trade change the number on a used Jeep or RAM in Ohio?

Ohio taxes a vehicle bought from a licensed dealer on price minus your trade allowance. That credit matters in Richland, Ashland, and Knox County as much as the allowance itself. If you still owe more than the trade is worth, the gap rolls into the new loan unless you pay it down.

Start with a written allowance on Value Your Trade. Keep the vehicle price and the trade number on separate lines so nobody buries one inside the other.

Why is a 2022 Cherokee on a list next to a 2025 Grand Cherokee L?

Different jobs. The 2022 Cherokee Limited and Trailhawk are the used bridge off the new 2026 Cherokee page. The Grand Cherokee L is the 3-row family unit. Open each VDP. Do not assume equipment carries across year or trim.

Need a new 2026 Cherokee instead? That page is New Jeep Cherokee near Butler. Need a new RAM? Use New RAM Dealer Select.
