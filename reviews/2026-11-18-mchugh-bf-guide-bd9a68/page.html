The short answer

If you're off for the holiday weekend, it can be an easy time to come see us. But no single day sets one price for every Jeep, Ram or Dodge. What you can get depends on the vehicle you choose and on the manufacturer’s programs that day. So instead of waiting for a headline, get your pieces in place and check the offers that are listed.

We are not publishing Black Friday prices in this article. Current offers live on our Manufacturer Specials, New Vehicle Specials and Used Vehicle Specials pages.

What can change in November

Manufacturer offers

Our Manufacturer Specials page organizes Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram offers by model year and model, and as of November 17, 2026 it lists model years from 2025 to 2027. Offers are shown per model and can have their own dates and eligibility rules, so open the page, filter to your vehicle and read the details.

Model-year timing

The same page now lists 2027 model-year offers for several models, including the Dodge Durango and Charger and the Ram 1500 and 2500, alongside 2026 and 2025 models. If you are comparing a 2026 with a 2027, ask which one matches your priorities and what the offers say for each.

Which vehicles are in stock

Browse Jeep, Ram and Dodge inventory, or look at used vehicles. See our End of Year Countdown Deals page too. Inventory changes daily, so confirm a vehicle is available before you drive over.

Four things to do before you visit

  1. Know your financing options. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau’s auto loan steps cover what to ask before you shop and how to compare ways to get an auto loan. When you are ready, our team can review a pre-approval application. You can also read about our finance department.
  2. Start your trade-in. Use the KBB Instant Cash Offer link on our site, and bring your title or loan information, keys and service records.
  3. Pick your short list. Decide on the model, cab or body style, and must-have options. If you want a three-row SUV or a truck, see the Durango America250 guide, the Grand Cherokee trims and inventory guide or the Ram 2500 guide.
  4. Ask for the full picture in writing. The CFPB says to make sure the paperwork matches the deal you think you are getting, and that everyone has signed all the documents, before you drive away. Ask what is included in the price and which offers apply to you.

Black Friday car-buying FAQ

Is Black Friday a good time to buy a Jeep, Ram or Dodge?

It can be, especially if you're off for the holiday weekend, but there is no single-day price for every vehicle. The offers on any Jeep, Ram or Dodge depend on that vehicle and the manufacturer’s programs that day. Check our Manufacturer Specials page for what is listed now.

Where can I see current Jeep, Ram and Dodge offers at McHugh?

Start with the Manufacturer Specials page, then the New Vehicle Specials and Used Vehicle Specials pages. We do not publish Black Friday prices in this article because offers change; those pages are the place to check.

Should I get financing pre-approved before I visit?

It helps you know your budget before you shop. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau recommends asking questions before you shop for a car or an auto loan and comparing loan options. You can start a pre-approval application with our finance team online.

Can I find out what my trade-in is worth first?

Our site has a KBB Instant Cash Offer link, so you can start a trade-in or sell-to-dealer offer before you visit. Bring your title or loan information, keys and service records when you come in.

What if the Jeep, Ram or Dodge I want is not on the lot?

Check Jeep, Ram and Dodge inventory first. If it is not there, our Custom Order page explains how to place a factory order.
